### 🚀 What I'm Currently Working On

#### 🌲 Forest Guardian
An intelligent, real-time forest protection and environmental monitoring system that combines **Edge AI, IoT, sensors, cameras, and automated alerts**. It detects and responds to threats such as forest fires, illegal activities, wildlife-related incidents, and environmental risks. Built around distributed field devices for intelligent processing at the edge!

#### 🔥 Gas Leak Protector
An **ESP32-based smart safety device** designed to detect LPG/gas leaks and automatically prevent accidents. Using an MQ-series gas sensor, it continuously monitors gas concentration. Upon detecting a leak, it activates a servo-based mechanism to shut off the gas supply valve and sends an **SMS alert via Twilio**.
